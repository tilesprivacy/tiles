

# Using AI to contribute to Tiles

Tiles is an AI product, and some people who maintain it use AI tools every day. You're welcome to use them too. What we ask is that a person stays responsible for everything sent to this project.

## The rules

**Understand what you send.** You should be able to explain your changes, and how it fits with the rest of Tiles, without asking the AI. When a reviewer has a question, they are asking you.

**Edit what the AI writes for you.** AI can help with issues, discussions and PR descriptions. AI is very good at being overly verbose and including noise that distracts from the main point. Humans must do their research and trim this down.

**Text and code only.** Don't submit AI-generated images, video, audio or other media.

## Why we have rules

Every issue, discussion and pull request is read by a maintainer. AI makes it cheap to produce work that looks finished and isn't, and the maintainers then face the problem of reviewing all those things.

They are not a stance against AI. The tools are fine. However, unreviewed output is not acceptable.

## If the rules aren't followed

We close contributions that are plainly unreviewed AI output, without a detailed review. People who keep sending them will be blocked from contributing.

If you're new and want to learn, we're glad to help. Do the work yourself, ask questions, and we'll review it with you.

## Who this applies to

These rules cover contributions from outside the project as well as the maintainers of the Tiles project. 

AI agents working in this repository should also read [AGENTS.md](./AGENTS.md).
